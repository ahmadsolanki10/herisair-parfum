An atmosphere that evolves

A conventional air freshener is designed to mask. Hérisair is composed to unfold. Its brighter notes create the first impression; its woods, musks, ambers and spices remain closer to the interior, revealing themselves over time.

The ritual

Begin with restraint. Place the fragrance as directed with the vessel secure and upright. Allow the composition time to settle into the cabin before adjusting intensity. A refined atmosphere should be discovered, never forced.

Made for intimate space

A vehicle amplifies scent differently from a room. Hérisair balances presence with composure so the fragrance accompanies rather than overwhelms.

Care in the UAE climate

Keep the vessel away from direct sunlight and extreme heat wherever possible. Never leave it unsecured, apply liquid to vehicle surfaces, or position it where it may affect visibility or driving controls. Store unused fragrance upright in a cool, dry place.
