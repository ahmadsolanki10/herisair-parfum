Fragrance care

The details

Essential guidance on use, longevity, gifting and choosing your chapter.

How do I begin using Hérisair?

Begin with a gentle shake and follow the directions on the reverse of the box. Mist lightly across the carpeted mats, then allow the fragrance to settle and reveal itself gradually through the cabin.

How long does the fragrance last?

Each application typically remains perceptible for four to six hours, often longer. Climate, cabin temperature and whether the car is sheltered or exposed to direct sun will naturally influence its diffusion and longevity.

Is it safe to keep Hérisair in the car?

Hérisair’s alcohol-free formulation is created for use within the cabin. To preserve its character and performance, we recommend storing the bottle somewhere cool and shaded rather than leaving it inside a hot vehicle or in direct sunlight.

Will the fragrance mark my interior?

Hérisair is water-based, but any fine mist may leave a temporary trace on certain finishes. We recommend applying it to removable carpeted mats, avoiding leather and polished surfaces, and testing discreetly first. Any light residue can usually be lifted with a clean, damp cloth.

Can I give Hérisair as a gift?

Hérisair was conceived as a gift of atmosphere and meaning—an elegant expression of Emirati culture for someone’s most personal space on the road.

Which scent should I choose?

Our scent discovery consultation considers your preferred atmosphere and notes, then recommends Unity, Ascent or Eminence.
