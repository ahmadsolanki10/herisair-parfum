How should I begin using Hérisair?

Secure the fragrance according to the instructions supplied with your product. Begin at the lowest intensity and allow the scent to settle before adjusting it.

How long does the fragrance last?

Longevity varies with temperature, ventilation, vehicle size and chosen intensity. Confirm the tested duration for the final product before publishing a fixed claim.

Is it safe in high temperatures?

Do not leave the vessel in direct sunlight or extreme heat. Keep it upright, secured and away from vehicle surfaces, controls and a driver's line of sight.

Will the fragrance mark my interior?

Do not apply fragrance liquid directly to leather, wood, textile, plastic or painted surfaces. Wipe any accidental contact promptly according to your vehicle manufacturer's care guidance.

Can I give Hérisair as a gift?

Every order is presented with care. Dedicated gift messaging and enhanced presentation can be introduced once fulfilment details are confirmed.

Which scent should I choose?

Our scent discovery consultation considers your preferred atmosphere and notes, then recommends Unity, Ascent or Eminence.
