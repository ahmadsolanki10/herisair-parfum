Your order

We want every Hérisair order to arrive in exceptional condition. Contact client care promptly if your order is damaged, incorrect or incomplete, retaining the product and all original packaging while the matter is reviewed.

Change-of-mind returns

For hygiene and product-integrity reasons, opened or used fragrance cannot normally be returned. Unopened products in their original, saleable packaging may be eligible within the return period confirmed at launch.

Damaged products

Include your order number and clear photographs of the parcel, packaging and product. If the claim is accepted, Hérisair will arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing time after approval depends on the payment provider and issuing bank.
